Overview

Founded in 2016 and headquartered in Singapore, Simpaisa is a technology company. We are the strategic monetisation partner to ride-hailing, gaming, media, digital commerce and marketplace businesses across MENA and South Asia, building the method connectivity, routing, reconciliation and settlement they rely on to get paid in every market they enter.

Our work covers merchant acceptance (C2B), disbursements (B2C) and remittance (C2C). A merchant integrates once, through a single API, and reaches the licensed banks, wallets, real-time payment systems and remittance partners of each market with routing, reconciliation and settlement structured for compliant fund flows and scheduled cross-border repatriation.

Simpaisa has held PCI DSS Level 1 and ISO 27001 certification since 2022 and was named Fastest Growing Fintech Company, Pakistan at the Global Banking & Finance Awards 2025.

What we enable

Merchant acceptance

Merchants collect payments through domestic wallets, bank transfers and real-time rails, domestic and international cards, and over-the-counter payments. One integration covers every market we operate in, with routing and failover between methods.

Local and cross-border payouts

Real-time disbursements to wallets, bank accounts and over-the-counter points, plus remittance payouts for partner networks. Funds are prefunded through the licensed partner in each market and paid out locally; the merchant receives settlement and reconciliation information in one place.
